# Identifying Key Drivers of Revenue for Zara Stores  
### *Data Analysis & Business Insights*  
**Capstone Project 2**

---

## Project Overview

Retailers like Zara face constant challenges in maximizing in-store sales and optimizing product performance. Store managers and merchandisers need clear, actionable insights to understand:

- Which products drive the most revenue  
- How pricing and promotions influence purchasing behavior  
- The impact of seasonality and product placement on sales  

This project analyzes Zara’s sales data to uncover key drivers of performance and provide insights that support better decision-making in inventory planning, marketing, and merchandising.

---

## Objectives

This analysis aims to answer the following business questions:

- Which product placements lead to higher sales?  
- Which products and categories generate the most revenue?  
- How does pricing impact sales performance?  
- Do promotions significantly boost sales?  
- How does seasonality affect product trends?
